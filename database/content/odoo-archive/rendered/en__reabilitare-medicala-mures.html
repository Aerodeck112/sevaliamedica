    MEDICAL REHABILITATION

    REABILITARE MEDICALĂ


    Medical rehabilitation is a complex process by which patients can regain function lost as a result of medical conditions or interventions. It includes personalized therapies to restore mobility, relieve pain and improve quality of life.

    If you need medical rehabilitation services in Mures, our clinic offers modern and personalized solutions for various conditions.

    Conditions treated

    Orthopedic conditions

    • Examples: fractures, sprains, dislocations, herniated disks.
    • Treatment: physiotherapy, physiotherapy, massage therapy.


    Neurological conditions

    • Examples: stroke, multiple sclerosis, neuropathies.
    • Treatment: coordination exercises, neuromuscular stimulation, electrotherapy.


    Post-surgery recovery

    • Examples: hip/knee replacement, orthopaedic surgery.
    • Treatment: individualized recovery programs, medical gymnastics, hydrotherapy.



    Rheumatologic conditions

    • Examples: arthritis, arthrosis, ankylosing spondylitis.
    • Treatment: manual therapy, anti-inflammatory procedures, mobility exercises.

    Cardiovascular diseases

    • Examples: post-myocardial infarction recovery, hypertension.
    • Treatment: adapted exercise, respiratory retraining, nutritional counseling.

    Frequently asked questions about medical rehabilitation

    What conditions require medical recovery?

    Any condition that affects mobility, muscle strength or motor functions can benefit from medical rehabilitation, from sports injuries to neurological diseases.



    Is medical rehabilitation painful?

    Most procedures are painless and therapies are tailored to minimize discomfort and provide an effective recovery.


    How often should I attend recovery sessions?

    The frequency varies depending on the condition, but 2 to 5 sessions per week is generally recommended.


    What type of exercise is recommended after treatment?

    After completing the rehabilitation program, it is recommended to continue with maintenance exercises, such as stretching, mobility exercises and muscle strengthening, as indicated by the specialist.


    When can I start medical recovery after surgery?

    Depending on the type of operation, recovery may begin a few days or weeks after surgery. Your specialist will advise you on the best time to start therapy.

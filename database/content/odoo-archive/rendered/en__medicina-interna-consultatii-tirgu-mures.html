    INTERNAL MEDICINE

    MEDICINĂ INTERNĂ


    Internal medicine is the medical specialty that deals with the prevention, diagnosis and treatment of disorders of the internal organs. Internists assess complex symptoms and offer solutions to multiple health problems, providing care to patients with chronic or acute illnesses.

    If you need an internist in Mures, our specialists are at your disposal for consultations and detailed investigations.

    Conditions treated

    Arterial hypertension

    • Symptoms: headache, dizziness, blurred vision, palpitations.
    • Causes: genetic factors, stress, unhealthy diet, sedentary lifestyle.
    • Treatment: lifestyle changes, antihypertensive medication, regular monitoring.


    Diabetes

    • Symptoms: excessive thirst, frequent urination, fatigue, weight loss.
    • Causes: genetic predisposition, unbalanced diet, obesity.
    • Treatment: personalized diet, oral medication or insulin, regular glycemic control.

    .

    Gastrointestinal diseases

    • Symptoms: heartburn, bloating, abdominal pain, diarrhea or constipation.
    • Causes: stress, poor diet, bacterial infections.
    • Treatment: balanced diet, specific medication, detailed investigations (abdominal ultrasound, endoscopy).



    Chronic respiratory diseases

    • Symptoms: persistent cough, shortness of breath, severe fatigue.
    • Causes: smoking, exposure to pollutants, recurrent infections.
    • Treatment: bronchodilators, anti-inflammatories, oxygen therapy if necessary.

    Anemias and metabolic disorders

    • Symptoms: chronic fatigue, pallor, dizziness, difficulty concentrating.
    • Causes: iron deficiency, chronic diseases, nutrient absorption problems.
    • Treatment: iron supplements, balanced diet, treatment of the underlying cause.

    Frequently asked questions about internal medicine

    When should I see an internist?

    If you have persistent symptoms, such as unexplained fatigue, frequent abdominal pain, palpitations or shortness of breath, it is advisable to schedule an internal medicine consultation in Mures.


    What tests are recommended in internal medicine?

    Depending on the symptoms, the doctor may recommend blood tests, abdominal ultrasound, electrocardiogram or other imaging investigations for an accurate diagnosis.


    What are the signs of high blood sugar?

    Excessive thirst, frequent urination, chronic fatigue and unexplained weight loss may indicate high blood glucose. It is important to consult an internist in Mures for a correct diagnosis.

    Care este diferența dintre un medic internist și un medic de familie?

    The family doctor provides primary care and general treatment, while the internist focuses on diagnosing and managing more complex diseases of the internal organs.


    What lifestyle is recommended to prevent metabolic diseases?

    Adopting a balanced diet, regular exercise, avoiding excessive intake of sugar and fat, maintaining an optimal weight and regular medical check-ups are essential to prevent metabolic diseases.

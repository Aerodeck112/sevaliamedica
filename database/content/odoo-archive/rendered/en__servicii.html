    OUR SERVICES

    Electrotherapy

    Electrotherapy is a component of physiotherapy which, in combination with other methods, helps in many conditions of the muscular system.

    Saline therapy

    Saline therapy, also known as halotherapy, has multiple objectives aimed at improving general health and well-being by using the therapeutic properties of salt.

    Kinetotherapy

    Kinetotherapy is a form of rehabilitation of the musculoskeletal system, which uses as its main resource the human being and body movement in the form of well-defined exercises.

    Stanger bath

    An innovative treatment based on low-intensity electric currents applied in water that helps reduce pain, relax muscles and improve circulation. Ideal for people with neurological or rheumatic problems.

    Galvanic baths

    Combination therapy with water and galvanic electric currents, used to relax muscles, stimulate circulation and reduce inflammation. It is recommended in cases of joint disorders or chronic pain.

    Underwater shower

    An underwater massage, applied with a powerful jet, which stimulates circulation, relaxes tissues and helps relieve muscle tension. Ideal for relaxation and physical recovery.

    Dry needling

    Dry needling works by inactivating myofascial trigger points - areas of the muscles that have become tense due to stress, incorrect posture or overuse.

    Magnetotherapy

    Therapy based on the use of magnetic fields to reduce inflammation, pain and stimulate cell regeneration. Effective in treating joint and rheumatic conditions.

    Thermotherapy

    A treatment that uses heat to relax muscles, reduce pain and stimulate circulation. Ideal for rheumatic conditions and chronic muscle tension.

    Lymphatic drainage

    A gentle massage technique that stimulates lymphatic circulation, reducing fluid retention and eliminating toxins from the body. Perfect for fighting edema and improving immunity.

    Ultrasound therapy

    A modern treatment that uses ultrasound to reduce pain, inflammation and accelerate tissue healing. Useful in cases of muscle or joint injuries.

    Laser therapy

    Non-invasive therapy that uses laser beams to reduce pain, accelerate healing and stimulate tissue regeneration. Recommended for chronic conditions or acute injuries.

    Massage

    A traditional relaxation and restorative technique ideal for reducing stress, relieving muscle aches and pains and improving circulation. Benefits both body and mind.

    Kinesio taping

    Kinesio taping is a modern therapeutic technique that uses special elastic bands strategically applied to affected areas to support muscles, reduce inflammation and improve function without restricting movement.

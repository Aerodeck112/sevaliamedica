    RHEUMATOLOGY

    REUMATOLOGIE


    Rheumatology is the branch of medicine that deals with the diagnosis and treatment of musculoskeletal and autoimmune disorders. These diseases can affect joints, bones, muscles and connective tissues, causing pain, stiffness and inflammation.

    If you experience joint discomfort or suffer from rheumatism, make an appointment with a rheumatologist in Mures for a correct diagnosis and personalized treatment.

    Conditions treated

    Rheumatoid arthritis

    • Symptoms: joint pain, morning stiffness, swelling, chronic fatigue.
    • Causes: autoimmune disease in which the immune system attacks the joints.
    • Treatment: anti-inflammatory drugs, biological therapies, physiotherapy.


    Arthritis

    • Symptoms: joint pain and stiffness, loss of mobility, joint deformities.
    • Causes: wear and tear of articular cartilage with advancing age, genetic factors.
    • Treatment: painkillers, hyaluronic acid infiltration, exercise.


    Ankylosing spondylitis

    • Symptoms: persistent back pain, lumbar stiffness, eye inflammation.
    • Causes: genetic predisposition, abnormal inflammatory reactions.
    • Treatment: anti-inflammatories, biological therapy, physiotherapy.



    Systemic lupus erythematosus

    • Symptoms: rash, joint pain, extreme fatigue.
    • Causes: autoimmune disorders, genetic and environmental factors.
    • Treatment: corticosteroids, immunosuppressants, sunscreen.

    Gout and pseudogout

    • Symptoms: intense joint pain (usually in the big toe), inflammation.
    • Causes: accumulation of uric acid (in gout) or calcium pyrophosphate (in pseudogout).
    • Treatment: medicines that reduce uric acid, balanced diet, hydration.

    Osteoporosis

    • Symptoms: frequent fractures, back pain, loss of height.
    • Causes: calcium and vitamin D deficiency, menopause, sedentary lifestyle.
    • Treatment: calcium and vitamin D supplements, exercise, drugs to increase bone density.

    Frequently asked questions about rheumatology

    When should I see a rheumatologist?

    If you have persistent joint pain, morning stiffness or frequent inflammations, it is advisable to consult a specialist for a correct diagnosis. At our rheumatology clinic in Mures, you will benefit from comprehensive consultations and personalized treatments.


    Is rheumatism hereditary?

    Some rheumatic diseases have a genetic component, such as rheumatoid arthritis or ankylosing spondylitis, but environmental factors also play an important role. A rheumatology consultation in Mures can help you find out if you are predisposed to such conditions.


    Are there natural remedies for joint pain?

    Although some supplements and diets can help relieve symptoms, medical treatments are essential to control the condition. Talk to a rheumatologist in Mures about the best options for you.


    What are the first signs of a rheumatic disease?

    Early symptoms may include persistent joint pain, morning stiffness, joint inflammation and chronic fatigue. If you notice these symptoms, a rheumatologic consultation is recommended.


    Can rheumatism be preventable?

    Although not all rheumatic diseases are preventable, a healthy lifestyle, balanced diet and regular exercise can help reduce the risk of developing them.


    What foods are recommended for joint health?

    Foods rich in omega-3 (salmon, nuts, flaxseed), green vegetables, antioxidant-rich fruits and foods containing vitamin D and calcium are good for joints.
